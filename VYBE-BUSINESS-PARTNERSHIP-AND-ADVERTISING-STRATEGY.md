# Purpose

This document defines how VYBE can create a business partnership ecosystem connecting:

* Businesses
* Creators
* Supporters
* VYBE

The goal is not traditional advertising.

The goal is creating authentic community-based partnerships.

# The VYBE Advertising Philosophy

Traditional advertising interrupts people.

Businesses are not simply buying impressions.

They are gaining access to trusted communities.

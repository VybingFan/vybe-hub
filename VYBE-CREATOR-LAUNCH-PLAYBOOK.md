# First 30 Days On VYBE

---

# Days 1-7

## Build Awareness

Goals:

Introduce VYBE.

Actions:

Post:

"I'm building something new."

"Join my VYBE community."

Share:

* Profile link
* First content
* Community purpose

---

# Days 8-14

## Create Engagement

Actions:

* Post updates
* Share exclusive content
* Respond to supporters
* Start conversations

Goal:

Make early supporters feel valued.

---

# Days 15-21

## Introduce Monetization

Do not immediately sell.

Provide value first.

Examples:

Create:

* Exclusive release
* Private update
* Special experience

Then introduce:

Membership opportunities.

---

# Days 22-30

## Grow The Community

Actions:

* Invite more supporters
* Highlight community members
* Share milestones

Examples:

"500 people joined my VYBE."

"Thank you to my founding supporters."

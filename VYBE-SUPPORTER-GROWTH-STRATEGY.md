# VYBE Supporter Growth Strategy

## Version 1.0

# Purpose

This document defines the strategy for attracting, engaging, and retaining supporters on VYBE.

Supporters are not simply users.

They are the foundation of every creator community.

VYBE succeeds when supporters move from:

Following creators

to

Belonging to communities.

# Supporter Value Proposition

## 1. Exclusive Access

Supporters receive access to:

* Early releases
* Behind-the-scenes content
* Creator updates
* Exclusive announcements
* Private experiences

The feeling:

"I get to see what others don't."

---

## 2. Real Connection

Supporters want creators to feel accessible.

VYBE enables:

* Creator posts
* Community conversations
* Live experiences
* Supporter interactions

The feeling:

"I am closer to the creator."

---

## 3. Recognition

Fans want their support to matter.

VYBE creates:

* Supporter badges
* Membership levels
* Founder recognition
* Community milestones

The feeling:

"The creator knows I am here."

---

## 4. Belonging

The strongest communities create identity.

Supporters should feel:

"This is my community."

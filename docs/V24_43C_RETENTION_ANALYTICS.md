# V24.43C Retention Analytics

Turns privacy-safe V24.43B playback sessions into creator-facing results.

## Creator Insights additions

- Qualified plays and unique qualified listeners
- Total and average listening time
- Completions and completion rate
- Repeat listeners
- 25%, 50%, 75%, and 90% listening thresholds
- Song-by-song performance table
- Creator self-plays excluded
- Membership reporting window enforced

Raw listener session identifiers remain unavailable to creators. Only owner-scoped aggregates are returned.

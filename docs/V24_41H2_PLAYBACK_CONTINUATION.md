# V24.41H2 Playback Continuation

## Listener flow

- Artist's Top 5 begins with the selected song, continues through the remaining ranked songs, and then moves into the same creator's eligible catalog.
- The listener may order the remaining creator catalog by added date, release date, or title.
- A song never enters automatic continuation unless it is published, public, selected for the public profile, playable, and backed by an authorized audio URL.
- Completed songs are tracked for the listening session and are not repeated.
- When the creator queue ends, playback stops. Similar creators, genre charts, and VYBE Top 50 remain clearly labeled next choices until ranking data and governance are ready.

## Privacy boundary

Private, unlisted, scheduled, archived, expired, membership-only, approved-listener, no-playback, and otherwise unauthorized tracks are never inserted into this public automatic queue.

## Later phases

1. Extend a public playlist with additional eligible music from the same creator after its saved sequence ends.
2. Add similarity ranking based on declared genre, mood, and listener behavior.
3. Publish governed all-genre and per-genre Top 50 charts.
4. Keep promoted placement visibly separate from organic ranking.

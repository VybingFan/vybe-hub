# VYBE Internal Copyright Operations SOP — Draft

Status: Attorney review and staff training required

## Intake and triage

1. Preserve the original notice, attachments, headers and receipt time.
2. Acknowledge receipt promptly using an approved template.
3. Verify required elements without deciding ownership merely from an allegation.
4. If incomplete, request the missing information and record the request.
5. If facially compliant, identify the exact material, creator and relevant systems.
6. Escalate threats, court papers, ownership conflicts, mass notices, suspected fraud and unclear fair-use issues to counsel.

## Access control

1. Record the original content state before changing it.
2. Disable access expeditiously when the approved reviewer determines action is appropriate.
3. Notify the creator promptly, identify the affected material and explain the counter-notice route.
4. Pause related sales and new fulfillment. Preserve orders, evidence and lawful accounting records.
5. Do not delete the evidence record merely because public access is disabled.

## Counter-notices

1. Review for required elements and identity/contact information.
2. Forward a compliant counter-notice to the original claimant and record proof of transmission.
3. Tell the claimant that restoration may occur under the statutory schedule.
4. Track the 10-to-14-business-day window. The software dates are reminders; staff must account for weekends, federal holidays and counsel’s instructions.
5. Do not restore if qualifying notice of a filed court action is received. Place the case on legal hold.
6. Restore only after an authorized reviewer completes the checklist.

## Strikes and repeat infringement

- A notice is not automatically a strike.
- Issue a strike only after documented review and record the reason.
- Reverse a strike when the underlying decision is reversed or VYBE determines it should not count.
- Three counting strikes within 12 months trigger suspension and termination review.
- Deliberate or serious infringement may justify earlier action; exceptional circumstances may justify a different documented result.

## Retention and security

Limit case access to authorized administrators and counsel. Preserve notices, counter-notices, decisions, delivery evidence, state snapshots, strikes, orders and legal holds according to an attorney-approved retention schedule. Do not place sensitive claimant or creator information in public logs.

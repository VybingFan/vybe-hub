# VYBE V24.39 Phase 1 — Creator Website and Public Music

## Purpose

V24.39 separates the creator's private catalog from the public creator website. Uploading or publishing a song no longer determines how prominently it appears on the creator's page.

## Core rules

1. A song is uploaded once and remains one master Music Library record.
2. Song access and public-page placement are separate controls.
3. Playlist access and public-page placement are separate controls.
4. Only published Public playlists may be selected for a creator website.
5. Unlisted, approved-listener, subscriber, private, draft, and expired playlist links are never listed publicly by this feature.
6. Playlist URLs and protected-sharing authorization remain unchanged.

## Public creator experience

- Artist-selected Top 5
- Latest Release
- Choose Your Vybe filters using genre and existing mood metadata
- Compact Releases and Songs section with progressive disclosure
- Creator-selected Public Playlists
- Website-style section navigation for Music, About, Videos, Shop, and Community
- Compact private Profile view without the duplicate Details card

## Creator controls

The new Public Music Setup workspace allows a creator to:

- show or hide eligible songs on the public creator page;
- assign each Top 5 position from 1 through 5;
- choose which eligible Public playlists appear on the page;
- preview the public creator page.

Assigning an occupied Top 5 position replaces the previous song in that position. It does not delete, unpublish, or remove the previous song from the Music Library.

## Discovery readiness

A creator is eligible for public Discovery when all of the following are present:

- VYBE username;
- artist or display name;
- meaningful biography of at least 40 characters;
- profile image;
- at least one genre;
- city or location;
- at least one published Public song.

The private Profile page displays the checklist. Public Discovery uses the same rules.

## Membership direction

This phase improves the Creator Plus baseline. Later phases will add membership-controlled Pro and Studio website modules such as creator logos, custom section order, businesses and projects, charities and community work, press, career history, advanced branding, custom domains, and team controls.

## Explicitly not included

- Payment processing or music commerce
- Pro/Studio website-module enforcement
- Custom domains
- Business and charity record editors
- Advanced page-theme controls
- Subscriber entitlement expansion

These remain separate phases so this public music foundation can be tested first.

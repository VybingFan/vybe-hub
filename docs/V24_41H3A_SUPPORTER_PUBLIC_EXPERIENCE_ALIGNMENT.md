# V24.41H3A Supporter Public Experience Alignment

VYBE should not give supporters a weaker duplicate of the public experience.
Signing in adds saving, following, preferences, community participation, My
VYBE, profile, and settings while preserving the working public discovery
catalog.

This correction maps:

- Discover to `/explore`, the real creator and music catalog.
- Listen to `/experience/listen`, the public listening destination.
- Mobile Discover to `/explore`.
- Supporter Merch to the existing public merchandise destination.

The V24.41H3 supporter interests, private profile home, and community
conversation foundations remain available. No database migration is added.

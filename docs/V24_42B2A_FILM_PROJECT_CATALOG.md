# V24.42B2A Film Project Catalog

## Purpose

This is the first full Film & Video workspace bundle after creator-focus authorization. It turns the earlier film-playlist draft foundation into an authorized workspace centered on actual film projects.

## Included

- `/film-studio` project workspace.
- Projects for features, shorts, documentaries, series, episodes, music videos, trailers, and other film/video work.
- Production stages from concept through release and archive.
- Draft synopsis, optional release year, poster URL, and official **Where to watch** destination.
- Finished and unfinished projects can coexist without being publicly released.
- Film project and film playlist writes require active Film & Video focus access.
- Project-count limits use the creator's film allowance.
- Private hosted delivery remains disabled until its security bundle is complete.
- The old film-playlist discipline selector is replaced with a link to Creator Focuses.
- Includes the earlier Film Playlist route and service when they are not already present, making this bundle self-contained.

## Why this is V24.42B2A

Project identity and authorization must exist before clips, scenes, trailers, private review links, music-matching requests, and film analytics can be reliably attached to the correct production.

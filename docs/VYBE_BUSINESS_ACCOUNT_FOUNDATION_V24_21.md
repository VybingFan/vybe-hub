# VYBE V24.21 - Business Account Foundation

V24.21 establishes businesses as first-class VYBE account holders.

## Added

- `business` application role and onboarding choice.
- Business-specific post-login route at `/business`.
- Business Studio navigation.
- Business partnership application and pending review state.
- One owned business profile per business account.
- Owner-only access to business profile, campaigns, offers, creatives,
  placements, and partner-visible documents.
- Protection preventing business users from changing verification, package,
  founding-partner, and other administrative review fields.
- Business access to the normal VYBE community experience.

## Release boundary

Business users can apply and review their account and campaign inventory.
Campaign creation, AI assistance, creative submission, and business-side
document uploads remain intentionally marked for the next Business Studio
release. Administrators continue to verify businesses in `/admin/businesses`.

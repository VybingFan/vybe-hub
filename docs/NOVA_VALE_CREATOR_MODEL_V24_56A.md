# Nova Vale — VYBE Model Creator Blueprint

## Purpose

Nova Vale is VYBE's transparent AI-assisted model creator. She demonstrates how a creator can turn a clear identity, authorized work, thoughtful discovery information, supporter access, stories, playlists, merchandise, events, community, and an EPK into one connected public destination.

Nova is not presented as an independently living human performer. She is an AI-assisted artist persona created and directed by a real woman rediscovering a creative voice she had set aside. Her human creator supplies the memories, point of view, poetry, direction, selection, revision, and final publishing decisions.

## Core story

Nova's human creator was born in New Orleans, where blues, jazz, gospel, family stories, and the musical cadence of ordinary conversation formed her earliest creative language. She moved to New York with her parents as a child, then was sent to Florida in her early teens during a period of growing pains. Those moves taught her how identity can be carried, challenged, and rebuilt across places.

She later returned to the Northeast and lived in New Jersey for several years. There, adulthood and motherhood moved to the foreground. She sang privately and filled notebooks with poems, verses, and unfinished songs, but responsibilities gradually displaced the practice of making music. A later move to Texas gave her the distance and room to reconsider the parts of herself she had postponed.

Then she raised children, worked, handled responsibilities, and became the person others depended on. Music became a memory instead of a practice. Years later, her children introduced her to AI music tools. Experimenting with those tools reopened a door she thought had closed. Nova Vale became her alter ego and creative mirror: a way to transform lived experience into country-soul songs about motherhood, reinvention, grown-woman love, family memory, faith, humor, regret, and second chances.

1. Public full lead song — discovery and immediate listening.
2. Public full secondary song — range without confusing the core identity.
3. Public preview — an authorized excerpt with a reason to continue.
4. Member song — available after creating a free VYBE account.
5. Follower song — rewards an intentional supporter relationship.
6. Unlisted song — excluded from profile and search; opened only through its direct link.

## Content and rights safeguards

- Use only music, images, writing, and products the human creator owns or is authorized to publish.
- Disclose material AI assistance consistently.
- Never expose private tracks or storage links in public demonstration code.
- Unlisted content must not appear in public discovery or profile lists.
- Never describe Nova as a touring human artist unless a real disclosed performer is attached.
- Do not invent awards, press coverage, sales, chart results, testimonials, or partnerships and present them as real.

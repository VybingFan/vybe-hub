# V24.41H3A1 Supporter Session Visibility

The public VYBE catalog is shared by visitors and signed-in supporters. This
release makes that distinction visible instead of sending supporters into a
duplicate discovery interface.

- Visitors see **Supporter Sign In** and **Create Free Account**.
- Signed-in members see their account name, **My VYBE**, and **Member Home**.
- Explore messaging describes the visitor or member experience accurately.
- The final Explore action panel changes from account creation to saved-content
  and member-home actions after sign-in.

No role, content, commerce, membership, or database records are changed.

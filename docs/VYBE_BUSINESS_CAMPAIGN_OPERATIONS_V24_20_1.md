# VYBE V24.20.1 - Business Campaign Operations

This bundle adds the administrator campaign workspace at
`/admin/campaigns/:campaignId`.

Administrators can:

- Move campaigns through operational statuses.
- Create and approve member offers.
- Create and approve sponsored creatives.
- Schedule and approve placement records.
- Maintain campaign document records.
- Review valid impression, click, and conversion totals.
- Review release-readiness gates.

Public advertisement rendering remains intentionally disconnected. A placement
must be connected to a public VYBE surface in a later controlled release.

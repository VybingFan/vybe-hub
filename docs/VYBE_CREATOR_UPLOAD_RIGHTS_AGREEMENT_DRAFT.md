# VYBE Creator Upload and Rights Agreement — Draft

Version: VYBE-CUR-2026-08-13
Status: Attorney review required before production acceptance

By uploading or publishing content, the creator certifies that:

1. They own the content or possess every written permission needed for VYBE to host, reproduce, process, stream, display, distribute, promote and—only when separately enabled—sell it.
2. They have accurately identified writers, producers, performers, featured artists and other contributors.
3. Samples, interpolations, beats, compositions, recordings, artwork, photographs, trademarks and other third-party materials are cleared for the uses selected on VYBE.
4. No label, publisher, distributor, collaborator, collective-management arrangement or other agreement conflicts with the upload or selected use.
5. They will maintain and provide licenses, split sheets, permissions and other evidence when VYBE reasonably requests it.
6. They will not re-upload disabled material or use another account to avoid a restriction.
7. They will cooperate with copyright notices, audits, payment disputes and investigations.

The creator grants VYBE a nonexclusive license necessary to operate the selected platform features. The final agreement must specify duration, territory, sublicensing to infrastructure providers, termination, surviving buyer entitlements, representations, indemnification, dispute procedures and governing law.

VYBE may request evidence; hide or disable content; pause promotion, downloads, sales or payouts; preserve records; issue or reverse strikes; suspend or terminate accounts; and respond to notices, court orders and legal obligations under its published policies.

This draft is a product requirement and is not legal advice. Qualified counsel must approve the final contract.

# V24.45A Entertainment Creator Taxonomy

V24.45A establishes the shared language future VYBE entertainment workspaces and discovery experiences will use.

## Nine creator focuses

1. Music — available.
2. Film & Video — foundation.
3. Acting & On-Camera Performance — planned.
4. Comedy — planned.
5. Theater & Live Production — planned.
6. Writing, Poetry & Storytelling — planned.
7. Dance & Choreography — planned.
8. Podcasting & Audio Entertainment — planned.
9. Visual & Digital Entertainment — planned.

Existing Music and Film access is preserved. Planned focuses remain locked and are not sold or activated by this bundle.

## Separate concepts

- A focus is a paid discipline workspace.
- A role describes what a creator does inside a focus.
- A discovery category describes how supporters find work.
- A credit records a contribution to another creator's work.

Roles, categories, and credits never grant membership or focus access.

## Foundation delivered

- Structured roles for all nine focuses.
- Shared Listen, Watch, Read, Live, Performance, Stories, Visual Stories, Community, and Merchandise discovery categories.
- Focus-to-discovery mappings.
- Public focus-readiness records with admin-controlled status.
- Cross-focus work credits with owner, credited-creator, public/member/private visibility, and acceptance status.
- Creator Focuses cards show roles, discovery placement, readiness, and locked planned workspaces.
- Back Office Focus Access shows the full taxonomy and readiness controls.
- Existing membership focus limits continue to control how many workspaces a creator may access.

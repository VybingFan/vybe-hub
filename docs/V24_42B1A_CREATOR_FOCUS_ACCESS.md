# V24.42B1A Creator Focus Access Foundation

Establishes creator-focus authorization before Film/Video and Theater workspaces expand.

## Rules established

- Every existing creator keeps one primary focus; existing accounts default to Music.
- `multidisciplinary` is removed as a pseudo-focus. Multiple real focuses require explicit access.
- Free supports one focus, Plus up to two, and Pro/Studio up to five.
- Founding Creator access is Pro-equivalent for focus-count eligibility while active.
- A creator may change the primary focus only to another already-authorized focus and must confirm.
- Changing the primary focus does not delete or move creator content.
- Additional access can be granted by a later subscription workflow or by an administrator.
- An active primary focus cannot be removed until another authorized focus becomes primary.
- Per-focus usage records are separated for later server-side enforcement.

## Pricing constants prepared, not billed

- Second focus: $8 monthly / $80 annual.
- Pro Multi-Focus, up to five: $15 monthly / $150 annual.
- Studio Multi-Focus, up to five: $20 monthly / $200 annual.
- The multi-focus subscription replaces, rather than stacks with, the second-focus add-on.

## Deliberately deferred

- Stripe products, checkout, webhooks, renewals, and cancellation handling (V24.42B1B).
- Creator-facing focus selector, purchase cards, and workspace navigation (V24.42B1C).
- Film/Video and Theater content tools (V24.42B2 and V24.42B3).

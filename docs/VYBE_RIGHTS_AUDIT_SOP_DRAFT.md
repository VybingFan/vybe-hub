# VYBE Rights Audit SOP — Draft

## Purpose

Confirm that public and commercial content has supportable ownership information without treating creators as infringers merely because they were selected.

## Selection

- Neutral random sample: approximately 5% of eligible public tracks, capped at 25 per monthly run.
- Risk-based selection: sale listing, complaint, prior counting strike, metadata conflict, suspicious re-upload or manual escalation.
- Never select based on race, ethnicity, gender, genre, popularity, viewpoint or another protected or irrelevant characteristic.

## Evidence requested

Request only evidence relevant to the recording: identity/authority; master ownership; writer and producer splits; contributor permission; beat license; sample/interpolation clearance; artwork permission; distributor/label authority; and copyright or PRO information when applicable.

## Procedure

1. Give a clear request, secure submission method and reasonable response date (normally 10 days).
2. Allow an extension for a documented reasonable need.
3. Keep evidence private and access-controlled.
4. Record approved, changes requested, escalated or closed with reasons.
5. An unanswered audit may cause a temporary upload, promotion or sales hold—not an automatic copyright strike.
6. Refer disputed ownership, complex licenses and possible fraud to counsel.

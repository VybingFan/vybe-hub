# V24.41H3 Supporter Experience & Discovery Repair

This release repairs the first supporter journey without changing creator,
business, or administrator content.

## Included

- Working Tune your interests destination with private, editable preferences.
- Working Start exploring, genre, artist-story, and community discovery actions.
- Dedicated supporter profile/home instead of creator-profile assumptions.
- Functional supporter-started community conversations with moderation status.
- Clear separation between starting a conversation and creating a community.
- Private-by-default preference storage protected by row-level security.

## Deliberately not included

- Open creation of entirely new communities.
- Replies, reactions, moderator assignment, or notifications.
- Automated recommendation ranking.
- Changes to creator membership entitlements.

Those require separate product rules and enforcement work after this repair is
validated.
